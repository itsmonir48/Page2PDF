About

Why Page2PDF?

As students, researchers, and developers, we often find valuable content scattered across the web. Bookmarks get lost, websites change, and reading on screens isn't always ideal.

Page2PDF was built to solve this problem. It intelligently extracts the meaningful content from any webpage — stripping away the noise of ads, navigation, and popups — and converts it into a beautifully formatted PDF that's perfect for offline reading, printing, or archiving.
